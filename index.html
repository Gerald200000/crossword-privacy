Privacy Policy

Scripture Crossword · Effective 28 October 2026

In short: the game keeps your progress on your own device and has no accounts. The developer does not collect, receive or sell any personal information. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.

This policy explains how the Scripture Crossword app ("the app") handles information. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Information kept on your device

The app saves the following on your phone or tablet only:

  • your puzzle progress, letters entered, hints and checks left, and best times;
  • your settings, such as the daily reminder time and the puzzle level you last chose.

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to the developer. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Daily verse reminders

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a daily reminder on your device showing the verse of the day. Reminders are created on your device; no information is sent anywhere to provide them. You can turn them off or change their time in the app, or turn off notifications in your device settings.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the app opens your device's own share menu with a verse card or a message. What you share, and with whom, is up to you. The developer does not receive anything you share.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob: an optional short video you can choose to watch for extra hints, and a banner on the home screen. To show and measure these ads, Google's software in the app may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system and language;
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps;
  • diagnostic information about how the ads perform.

Google uses this information to deliver ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, to personalise ads. It is sent to Google over encrypted connections. The developer does not receive this information. Ad requests are marked for a teen audience and limited to content suitable for families.

Learn more in how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy settings on the app's home screen.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Settings under Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).
  • On iPhone and iPad, you can limit ad tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

What the app does not collect

The app has no sign-in or accounts. It does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location, and the developer does not run any servers that store information about you. The developer does not sell personal information.

Children

The app is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and you have concerns, please contact us at the address below.

Keeping and deleting information

Information on your device is kept until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Information collected by Google for ads is kept and handled under Google's own policies; you can reset your advertising ID at any time as described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
